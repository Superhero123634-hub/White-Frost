*White Frost*: Refrigeration Tools, Spares and Cold Rooms, Gandhidham, Kutch
About us
White Frost is a refrigeration supplier based in Gandhidham, Kutch. We supply the parts, tools and equipment that refrigeration businesses depend on, from a single relay to a complete plant. Our customers include technicians, contractors, shop owners and businesses that need cold storage.
What we supply
Compressors: hermetic, semi-hermetic and scroll types, for repair and replacement.
Condensing units: air and water cooled, from small shops to large stores.
Evaporators and coils: unit coolers, fans, heaters and defrost parts.
Controls and valves: thermostats, controllers, solenoid and expansion valves, and pressure switches.
Refrigerant gases and service tools: refrigerants, gauges, vacuum pumps, and flaring and brazing kits.
Spare parts: capacitors, relays, motors, filter driers and the small components that can stop a whole system.
Cold rooms
We supply cold rooms as one complete system: panels, doors, refrigeration equipment and controls. Each room is sized to your space and the load you need to hold, so there is one point of contact from requirement to delivery. This suits cold storage for food, produce and other goods that need a controlled temperature.
